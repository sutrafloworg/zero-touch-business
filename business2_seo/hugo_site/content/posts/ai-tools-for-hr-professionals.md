## How to Choose the Right AI Tool for Your HR Needs

Start by auditing which tasks consume the most hours each week. For most teams, that's writing (job posts, emails, templates), screening (resume review, skill matching), or analytics (trend spotting, retention prediction). Match your pain point to the tool's strength: Notion AI for documentation-heavy workflows, HireEZ if sourcing is the bottleneck, Textio if postings underperform, Paradox if screening takes forever.

Second, test within your actual workflow before committing. Most of these tools offer free tiers or trials. Spend one week using only the free version and measure actual time saved — not estimated savings. Real impact will be obvious in 5-7 days.

Finally, consider integration requirements. If you're heavily invested in one HRIS (Workday, ADP, BambooHR), prioritize tools with native connectors over general-purpose AI. API integrations work but add implementation friction that most HR teams underestimate.
