Start by identifying which tasks consume the most time: Are you spending hours on client reports (Notion AI is your answer)? Drafting compliance documents (Finbot)? Writing market commentary (Copy.ai or Rytr)? Next, assess your firm's compliance requirements—if you're a registered advisor, tools with audit trails and compliance awareness (MoneyGPT, Finbot) are non-negotiable. Finally, test free tiers before committing: most tools offer 7-30 day trials or free monthly allowances. I recommend starting with Notion AI (free tier) or Rytr (free tier) to get comfortable with AI-assisted workflows, then adding specialized tools based on what bottlenecks remain.
