## How to Choose the Right AI Tool for Your Business

Start by mapping your time-draining activities: content creation, customer communications, data entry, scheduling, reporting. Each tool I tested excels in specific lanes—Writesonic owns content, Notion owns documentation, HubSpot owns sales processes, Zapier owns workflow automation.

Next, assess your existing tool stack. If you're already in Notion, adding Notion AI makes more sense than learning a new platform. If you need customer relationship management, HubSpot's integrated AI is more coherent than bolting separate tools together.

Finally, test before committing. Every tool here offers meaningful free tiers—I spent 2-3 days with each before paying. Small businesses don't have budget for expensive mistakes, so use that free period to measure actual time savings. Multiply your hourly rate by the hours saved weekly, and you'll know if the paid tier makes sense.
