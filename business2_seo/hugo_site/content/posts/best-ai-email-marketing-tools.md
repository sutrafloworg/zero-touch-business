## How to Choose the Right Tool

Start by answering three questions:

**What's your primary bottleneck?** If it's copy quality, Rytr wins. If it's campaign coordination across channels, choose Brevo. If it's planning, use Notion AI.

**Do you need end-to-end automation?** Brevo and HubSpot handle everything. Rytr and Notion require external tools (Zapier, Make) for automation connections.

**What's your subscriber count?** Under 5,000? The free tiers of Brevo or HubSpot are sufficient. Over 50,000? You'll hit paid plans regardless, so optimize for integration quality instead of price.

My personal workflow: I use Rytr for initial copy, HubSpot for B2B sequences, and Brevo for product launches. Notion AI sits at the beginning for brainstorming. This combination costs roughly $40/month but has reduced my email production time by 60%.
